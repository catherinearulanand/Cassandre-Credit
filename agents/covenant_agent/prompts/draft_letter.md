Draft a short, formal reservation-of-rights letter to the borrower about a financial covenant breach.
Use only the facts provided under `facts`. Quote any figure exactly as given; never compute, round,
convert or estimate a number. Do not promise a waiver, a cure extension or any leniency. State that
the bank reserves all its rights. Do not mention internal scores or case identifiers.
